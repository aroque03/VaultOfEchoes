The Vault of Echoes

Wren — your mentor, the village mapmaker — vanished three weeks ago. For decades, Wren's charts kept every traveler safe. When the disappearances started, Wren went into the vault beneath the old keep to map a way through. Nobody has seen Wren since.

You are Wren's apprentice. You stand at the vault's mouth with Wren's last words still in your ears: "If I don't come back, don't follow me." You're going in anyway.

Three chambers lie ahead. Somewhere inside, Wren left journal pages — find them all and you may learn what happened. Hidden rooms, gems, and guarded trophies reward those who explore thoroughly.

Guards pace the corridors — each hit costs one of your 3 health points. Press Space to swing your sword.

How to Play

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD — Move
Space — Swing sword
B — Report a bug

Goal

Find the key in each chamber and reach the exit door to advance. There are 3 chambers total.

Scoring — max 4,200 points

Collect gems — 3 appear per level, blinking in and out of the corridors. Grab them before they vanish. Defeat enemies to earn a trophy. Points scale with each chamber, so later levels are worth more.

Beware: this game contains bugs!

Some things may not work as expected. When you encounter a bug, press B to open the bug report form. Describe what happened, select a category, and rate how much the bug impacted your enjoyment. Your reports are part of the study — report every bug you find.
